01 What it is

What is the Holographic Invisible Screen?

Built on a hollow, mesh-pattern circuit PCB with a fully borderless structure. At under 3 mm thick it can be bent, cut to size, and bonded onto transparent glass until screen and glazing read as a single surface.

Proprietary driver ICs keep perceived transparency above 80% across the range — up to 93% on P10. High pixel density renders fine detail at close range, with 60 fps playback and high-refresh 3D at 120 fps, so content appears to float on the glass.

02 Core Features

Why teams specify it

Under 3 mm Thin

Next to a coin, the panel edge almost disappears. Bonded to glass it reads as part of the glazing itself.

Light to Handle

From 1.2 kg per module — panels carry to site and onto the glass without lifting gear.

The hollow mesh circuit preserves the architecture and its daylight — content stops, the screen vanishes.

07 FAQ

Questions, answered

Holographic or Crystal Film — which one do I need?

Close viewing (2–6 m) or 3D content → Holographic. Large spans, budget priority, or longer viewing distances → Crystal Film. We'll confirm against your drawings.

Does it support 3D content?

Yes — high-refresh playback at 120 fps gives holographic-style floating 3D on the glass.

Can a failed LED be repaired?

Yes, single beads are repairable in place; repair video and guidance are provided.

Can it be used outdoors?

IP20 — indoor and in-glazing use; it displays outward through the glass.

What's the minimum order and lead time?

Per-project quotes, no fixed MOQ. Standard modules typically ship within 2–3 weeks.
