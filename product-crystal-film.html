01 What it is

What is the Crystal Film LED Screen?

Crystal Film is a self-adhesive, flexible transparent LED display: driver-integrated Mini-LED beads encapsulated in a transparent film just 2.5 mm thick. A proprietary adhesive-potting process gives the film its own bond — it applies straight onto glass with no steel structure and no drilling, and plays the moment power and signal are connected.

With transparency above 90%, the screen is close to invisible when off and never blocks daylight. Patented invisible-grid circuitry with in-bead driver ICs delivers industry-leading clarity, and a 6 cm minimum bend diameter lets the film follow columns, arcs, and shaped glazing.

07 FAQ

Questions, answered

Can you see the screen when it's off?

Transparency is 90%+ — from about three metres away the film is close to invisible, and it doesn't reduce interior daylight.

Do I need a steel structure or drilling?

No. The film bonds to the glass with its own adhesive; only the small power box needs fixing.

How much power does it draw?

200 W/m² on average, 600 W/m² peak (full-white at maximum brightness). Typical video content runs far below peak.

Can it be applied to curved glass?

Yes — minimum bend diameter is 6 cm, so cylinders and arcs are routine.

What happens if something fails?

Single-point faults are bypassed automatically without blacking the screen, and individual beads can be repaired in place.

How do I publish content?

Nova or Colorlight control systems, synchronous or asynchronous, published from a PC or phone.

Can it be used outdoors?

The film mounts on the inside of the glass and displays through it (semi-outdoor). UV-stable for 5–10 years.

What's the minimum order?

We quote per project — no fixed MOQ. Send your wall size and we'll spec the right system.
