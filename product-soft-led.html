01 What it is

What is the Soft LED Display?

A bendable, flexible LED matrix sign: a 16-dot-high LED grid in a soft body that conforms to window glass, walls, even curved surfaces. It runs on 5 V USB or Type-C power and draws just 10 W — about as much as a light bulb.

Pair over Bluetooth with the iPixel Color app (iOS / Android) or use the bundled remote to publish scrolling text in any language, pixel art, animations, clocks, and music-reactive effects. Waterproof build, and no installation work at all — unbox, stick, pair, play.

Unbox to live in five minutes

Unbox and stick or hang the soft panel on your window or wall — no tools needed.
